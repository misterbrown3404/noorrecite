## Information used by the app

Bookmarks, reflection notes, reading history, settings, playlists, cached coordinates and downloaded audio are stored on your device. The app does not currently offer account registration or cloud synchronization.

With your permission, foreground location is used to calculate prayer times on-device. Coordinates are cached for subsequent calculations. The device's geocoding service is used to resolve a place name and may process coordinates under its provider's terms. Background location is not requested.

QariVerse contacts MP3Quran to retrieve reciter information and stream or download audio. Those servers receive network information such as your IP address and the requested content. Review the service's privacy practices separately. Sharing a verse sends the text to the app or recipient you choose through the system share sheet.

Notifications are scheduled locally with your permission. Prayer and Azkar schedules are refreshed in a rolling seven-day window when the app runs. Daily Ayah reminders recur at 09:00 and 18:00 device-local time. You may disable reminders in Settings or system settings.

The inspected application code does not initialize advertising, analytics or a remote crash-reporting service. Recheck this statement whenever dependencies or services change.

## Retention and deletion

Local records remain until removed by you or app data is cleared. Individual bookmarks and downloads can be removed in their respective screens. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local app records, subject to operating-system behavior. Do not describe the current in-app reset as deleting every downloaded file: that implementation needs further work.

## Choices

Location and notification permission can be revoked through system settings. Reading bundled Quran and Azkar content does not require location. Network recitations require connectivity unless already downloaded.
