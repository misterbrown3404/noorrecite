## Full description

Make room for Quran in your day with QariVerse.

Read Quran with translations, save verses, and keep personal reflections. Browse reciters, stream recitations, and download surahs for offline listening. Follow your reading progress with daily goals and a weekly activity chart.

Explore morning and evening Azkar, duas, and the 99 Names of Allah. Calculate prayer times for your location and choose local reminders for prayers, daily Ayah, and remembrance.

Location is optional and used for prayer calculations. Notifications require permission. Prayer and Azkar reminders are scheduled for the next seven days and refreshed when you use the app. Some content and features require an internet connection.
