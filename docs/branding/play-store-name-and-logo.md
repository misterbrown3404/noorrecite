# Play Store naming and logo concept

Recommended title: **QariVerse: Quran & Prayer** (25 characters).

Alternatives: **QariVerse: Quran Audio** (22 characters), **QariVerse: Read Quran** (21 characters).

The recommendation preserves the existing brand and describes the app using relevant Quran and prayer terms. This is a naming judgment, not measured keyword-volume research or a ranking guarantee. No trademark or name-availability clearance has been performed. Google Play limits app titles to 30 characters: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9898842

The suggested name remains a concept. The generated logo is now applied to assets/images/logo.png and the Android and iOS launcher icons; the app name is unchanged.

Flat vector-like geometric design with confident clean curves and thick readable strokes, very minimal, premium and serene. Deep emerald background matching #153F3A to #091F25, warm gold #F4D49B emblem. Full bleed square background, do not bake rounded outer corners into the image. Emblem within central 65 percent safe zone, legible at small app icon sizes. No text, no letters written separately, no Arabic calligraphy, no mockup, no border, no watermarks, no shadows, no photographic texture. Output one square logo image.
